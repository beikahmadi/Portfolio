Pricing depends on the number of pages, design needs and features. Describe your project and I'll suggest a practical scope with a quote before any work begins.

Yes. We can review what is working, identify what needs improvement and decide whether a refresh or a full rebuild makes more sense.

No. I'm based in British Columbia and can collaborate remotely with businesses anywhere in Canada.

Yes. We can discuss updates, maintenance or improvements as part of a project scope or a separate support arrangement.
